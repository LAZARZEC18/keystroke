    Questions

    How is WPM worked out?
    WPM = (characters in correctly typed words, including the space after each one ÷ 5) ÷ minutes. Five characters count as one word, which is the usual standard. A word with any mistake left in it adds nothing.
    What is raw WPM?
    Raw counts every key you pressed that produced a character or space, right or wrong, the same way. If raw is far above WPM, you are losing speed to errors rather than to slow fingers.
    What does consistency mean?
    It compares your raw speed from second to second. 100% means you kept exactly the same pace the whole way; lower numbers mean bursts and pauses.
    Why can't I backspace into the previous word?
    Once you press space, that word is locked in. This keeps the score honest and matches how most typing tests work. You can still fix anything inside the word you are on.
    Where are my results stored?
    In your browser's storage on this device only. Nothing is uploaded. You can clear your history at any time from the Progress tab.
